# Inherited opportunity, not a blank starting line

Owner's family narrative, 24 October 2026, informs the mechanisms below. This note
intentionally excludes identifiable family history and sensitive allegations.
The narrative is testimony, not a verified historical dataset or a population rule.

## Simulation contract
- A newborn inherits a context: household security, obligations, location, available
  care, language and networks. These are not intelligence or moral worth.
- Earlier generations' migration, work and accumulated support can change those
  conditions. A network can open a door, impose obligations or enable exploitation;
  it never guarantees survival or advancement by ethnicity.
- Encounters can change aspirations: a visit, teacher, book or exchange may reveal
  a previously unseen path. Motivation changes opportunities pursued, not innate IQ.
- Effort, resources, eligibility, institutional access and uncertainty jointly shape
  education and work. Scholarships require actual criteria and recorded outcomes;
  a planned course is not a degree. Family help is visible alongside individual effort.
- Housing and security can release time and attention for learning. Compare both
  personal achievement and the support that made it possible; no single merit score.
- Relationships need reciprocity, consent and boundaries. Coercion is harm, never
  justified by cultural context. Do not turn a private family account into a generated
  scene or public biography.

## Delivery boundary
Current version records household means, personal finance, schooling/work spans,
projects and chosen relationships. It does not yet simulate ancestral migration,
network access, aspiration changes, admissions or scholarships. Implement those
with explicit sources and authored probability rules before claiming coverage.
