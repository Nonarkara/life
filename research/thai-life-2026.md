# Thai life circumstances: evidence and game boundaries

These five encounters are fictional composites. The sources support the
circumstances, not the simulated prices, age windows, frequency, skill gains,
wellbeing changes or eventual outcomes. Those values are authored game tuning.
New characters are not claimed to be real interview participants. Five-year
stations compress time; they do not measure the duration of a real negotiation.

## Political disruption and a journey home

[Thai MFA's archived announcement](https://www.mfa.go.th/en/content/5d5bcdc115e39c3060012119?cate=5f1a9f2340789f61121eca84)
records that the military council seized administration on 22 May 2014 and
invoked nationwide martial law. The [Australian Embassy release of 23 May 2014](https://thailand.embassy.gov.au/bkok/PR2014_Military_Coup.html)
documents the nationwide 22:00–05:00 curfew. Both were checked on 4 November 2026.
These are dated historical evidence, not a probability distribution or a
prediction of the next coup. The game prompt explicitly identifies an invented
chapter informed by 2014. It neither asserts a coup occurred in the player's
current scenario year nor assigns arrests, death or political virtue to a choice.
The practical fork is money for an earlier trip, schedule negotiation, or sharing
a journey. Normal support and coordination have costs without inventing a crime.

## Credit and crop sales can overlap

The [NSO Agricultural Census 2023, table 16](https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Census_Survey/Agricultural-Census-2566/52/)
includes middlemen among sources of agricultural credit. They account for 0.5%
of agricultural holders reporting borrowing (multiple sources allowed) in that table; BAAC accounts for 52.8%. A respondent could
report more than one source. This confirms that trader credit exists, not that
all farmers borrow from traders or that all rural poverty is caused by middlemen.
The buyer holding an input bill is an authored specific family circumstance.
It is offered only to a recorded rice-farming birth household, not on the basis
of a low-class label. A player contribution is an actual personal expense;
reading a bill or comparing terms does not erase principal. The game does not
invent a loan asset, transfer the family's liability onto the player, or promise
another buyer or lender will accept the family.

## Markets and water constrain the possible choices

The [World Bank Thailand Rural Income Diagnostic](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/thailand/publication/thailand-rural-income-diagnostic-challenges-and-opportunities-for-rural-farmers)
identifies agricultural productivity, crop diversification and access to markets
as constraints. It discusses irrigation, weather-risk mitigation, finance,
connections between farms and markets, and farmer learning. This supports the
shared truck and dry-season situations. It does not calibrate transport or water
prices, predict crop yields, or guarantee a gain from changing buyers or crops.
The family is introduced as asking for help; the protagonist is not silently
made a farm owner. Farming-origin lives that moved remain able to face a family
request, while non-farming origins do not receive it merely because they are poor.
No new crop-sale income is fabricated. The truck contribution and water payment
are bounded expenses through the existing ledger.

## A placement costs unequal shares of different buffers

The [World Bank's Bridging the Gap: Inequality and Jobs in Thailand](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/thailand/publication/bridging-the-gap-inequality-and-jobs-in-thailand)
describes early and persistent inequality of opportunity, including differences
in educational progression and access to later opportunities. It supports
showing the same cost consuming different shares of people's resources. It does
not establish the prevalence, price or effectiveness of unpaid placements.
The placement, paid local task and negotiation are explicitly authored examples.
Intrinsic intelligence does not depend on class; no choice guarantees a job.
Only the selected paid-placement branch may supply its modest five-years-later
practice callback. It must be registered with the existing lesson consequence
lookup to run; an unused `later` property is not an implemented payoff.

## Useful next evidence, not mechanics silently added here

[PIER's 5 May 2026 synthesis](https://www.pier.or.th/en/blog/2026/t092/)
discusses limited repayment capacity, payment schedules mismatched with actual
income, transaction costs, interest-only servicing, and differentiated debt
solutions. These support a future repayment-timing choice only when actual debt
is recorded. The current encounter cash-flow helper does not change principal.
A loan or principal repayment therefore needs a real debt-ledger operation,
validation, affordability and replay tests before it can be offered. Advice does
not cure structural debt, and declining to contribute does not make a person
less moral. No rates or frequencies in this source are imported as game odds.
