# Decision rhythm ·29October2026

Observed original0.8.80: ordinary annual life, contextual focus actions, a queued
school dilemma that takes focus and stops at age16, immediate education/focus
changes after answering, and a recorded path. These observations suggest useful
engagement mechanisms; they do not establish why the product went viral.

Life2.0 has meaningful authored branches but displays the station as a worksheet:
multiple questions at once, little acknowledgment after a pivot, and a supposedly
stopped incident while actors continue at20x. Fix presentation and time ownership
before changing the model or promising more extreme outcomes.

Deliver: one focused dilemma at a time; full context/alternatives/costs retained;
actual outcome and applied deltas acknowledged before the next dilemma; explicit
five-year commitments, never invented future outcomes. Native reading surface can
be set aside to inspect the life. Freeze for unanswered questions/incidents/outcome,
slow scene at five-year planning, ease fast travel into its last year. The existing
one advance action still commits the plan; no silent/default answer. Keep seeds,
saves, all catalogs, art, source qualifications and supported languages.

Validate double-answer guard, wrong-age/locked option rejection, reduced-motion
and fast transport boundaries, real pause/read/choose/outcome/next/review/commit
flows, reload preservation, desktop/phone/native type. A decision may temporarily
be the focal subject; its surface must fit its actual content without fixed-height
padding. Retention/player enjoyment require human testing, not green unit tests.
