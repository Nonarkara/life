# Childhood development notes · 29 October 2026

This register separates sourced developmental information from fictional family
circumstances and simulation assumptions. The game simulates a life, not a body.
Its health, wellbeing, reasoning and emotional-skill numbers are game indices,
not clinical measurements. No brain measurements are inferred from those indices.

## What changed in this audit

The unfinished draft included a brain-weight ratio, a claim that a newborn was
almost completely wired, and deterministic claims about family resources and
personality. Those claims were removed rather than presented without evidence.
The replacement notes are short summaries tied to the sources below.

The displayed childhood ages organize this fictional biography. They are not
ages at which every child must acquire a skill. A family may move between homes;
the developmental note remains present without diagnosing that child's response.

## What the first decade does

The first decade is watched rather than controlled. The family's decisions,
its scenario income and a brief developmental note appear alongside the world.
Pause and the existing previous-year control allow closer reading.

The pending change shortens the pacing constant from twenty to 3.5 seconds per
simulated year, before the transport-speed multiplier. That is a gameplay choice,
not a claim about development. Faster transport deliberately reduces reading time.
This change does not add a developmental score, genetic equation or new life event.

## Nature, nurture and agency

Genes and environments interact. The game does not estimate a percentage of a
person's intelligence, emotional life or temperament that comes from either one.
Nor does a point spent on reasoning imply a biological loss of physical strength.
The allocation is a game budget for effort and trade-offs.

Family, school, neighbourhood, chance and available resources shape the scenarios.
They do not determine what a real child will become. Player choices are a game
mechanic, not a claim that agency sits outside biology or environment.

## Books named in earlier direction

Steven Pinker's *The Blank Slate* (2002) was named in the owner's brief. It is
**not yet read into this register**. No childhood sentence is attributed to it.

The earlier unfinished draft recorded Rolf Dobelli's *The Art of Thinking Clearly*
(2013) as supplied and read. This resumption has not verified the supplied copy or
passages, so it does not treat that report as source evidence. The idea of showing
paths not taken remains a design intention; it is not proof of a book's contents.
The existing counterfactual is preserved without new claims of academic validation.

## Not claimed

No diagnosis, clinical scoring, fixed developmental deadline, brain-weight ratio,
IQ prediction, causal effect size or guarantee of a future outcome is provided.
The fictional swimming and cycling scenes are narrative, not safety instruction.
The age labels are not a developmental assessment. Book titles are not substitutes
for checking a source, and unretrieved passages must not be presented as read.

## Future edits

Add a source for each new factual note before translating or shipping it.
Keep the complete qualification when shortening text. Mark fiction and game
assumptions explicitly; do not silently promote them into biological claims.
