# Colour source and production roles

Intent: colour follows recorded life events; choices remain readable in every state.
Relationship: luminous warm/green chords for gains; indigo, violet, slate and earth
chords for setbacks; quieter cool/warm chords for ordinary intervals.
Chord: thirty documented three-colour combinations, named in palette-chords.js.
Roles: darkest member carries text; lightest carries reading surfaces; third marks
atmosphere and action. Dark instruments invert text onto the same darkest member.
World materials derive from the whole chord; sad scenes lower material lightness.
Budget: reading and world carriers dominate; action colour is sparse.
Risks: harmony does not guarantee contrast, especially on small labels or dark bars.
Proof: test actual text/background role pairs, complete scenario coverage, recorded
sequence changes, old saved pin ignored, real browser scene/console/settings changes.

Combinations and digital conversions copied from local Palette/data/colors.json,
which credits mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations and dblodorn/sanzo-wada.
These are documented digital combinations, not exact printed colours. Mood
assignments and material mixes are Life2.0 art direction, not claims by Sanzo Wada.
