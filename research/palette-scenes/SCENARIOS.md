# Scenario colour assignments

Two documented combinations per scenario. Opening uses the authored tone; recorded outcomes choose the pressure or recovery member. Ordinary periods use quiet chapter chords. These are game art direction, not psychological measurements. Critically low health/wellbeing overrides cheerful opportunities. The currently presented question owns its moment; travel retains the last recorded outcome until the next incident.
