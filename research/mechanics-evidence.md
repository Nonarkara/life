# Life v1.0 — mechanics and human-development evidence

Research receipt, 23 October 2026. This is source research, not a claim to have played every referenced game or exhausted 1in8b. Life is the 2026 release under development; the novel supplies a service premise, not evidence that immersion hardware exists. Proposed adaptations below are design hypotheses to test in Life. Developer accounts establish intention and implementation, not measured causes of commercial success.

## Six patterns worth adapting

| Pattern and primary source | What the source establishes | Proposed Life adaptation and test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergent stories with authored support — Maxis/EA's Matt Brown, [GDC 2018 session description](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1025112/) | The Sims combines player authorship, structured narrative and unexpected systemic stories. Only the indexed session abstract was accessible; the talk itself redirected to maintenance. | Give each person persistent relationships, needs and routines. An illness interrupts an existing commitment, rather than dropping an unrelated tragedy into a feed. Test whether players can recount a particular person and obligation after a run. This is an inference, not a reconstruction of Sims AI. |
| Context-sensitive event selection — Reigns creator François Alliot, [2016 first-person deep dive](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-creating-an-adaptive-narrative-in-i-reigns-i-) | Events are filtered against current state, weighted, and excluded when recently used; choices can enable related stories. Simple decisions acquire meaning through consequences. | Keep an authored event library with prerequisites, exclusions, cooldowns and follow-ups. Pregnancy needs the appropriate biological/story conditions; firing requires employment. Record why an event was eligible. Test impossible-event rejection and repetition across seeds. Do not imply that mere chronological adjacency proves causation. |
| Allocate known capacity against uncertainty — Citizen Sleeper creator Gareth Damian Martin, [2023 interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/how-citizen-sleeper-was-inspired-by-tabletop-rpgs-and-gig-work) | Daily dice are rolled before allocation to express limited capacity. | Show available effort and broad risk before committing. A five-year plan sets direction; significant events can open a shorter decision, so marriage or treatment is not decided by a blind five-year jump. Test whether players can explain what they knowingly risked. No invented clinical probability. |
| Visible progress and success with complications — [same creator interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/how-citizen-sleeper-was-inspired-by-tabletop-rpgs-and-gig-work) | Clocks and successes carrying costs support anticipation and complications. Ordinary work can lead to meaningful relationships and stories. | Use small visible progress arcs for qualification, rehabilitation, a relationship or a project. Earning money may consume time promised to someone. Failure can redirect a project instead of merely subtracting happiness. Test whether a low-wealth life can still have meaningful, distinctive progress. |
| Optional challenge reframes familiar systems — ZA/UM's [23 January 2020 studio announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=632470&enddate=1579876682&feed=steam_community_announcements) | Disco Elysium's optional Hardcore mode changes check difficulty, available money, medicine and the importance of existing equipment/thoughts, offering experienced players a different perspective. | Let players choose an authored starting circumstance and later replay the same world with another plan. Do not make dark events the universal definition of depth. Test meaningful branch differences, not just higher damage. The cited announcement does not by itself establish a general fail-forward narrative theory. |
| Agency, mastery and connection — Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski, [2006 original four-study paper](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf) | Perceived autonomy and competence were associated with enjoyment; a multiplayer survey also linked relatedness to enjoyment and future play. These studies do not prove a specific Life mechanic will cause retention. | Let players set goals, understand effects, and care about recurring people. Ask whether a choice felt meaningful, whether its effect was understandable, and whom they cared about. Voluntary replay is useful evidence; session duration alone is not. |

Candidate loop: observe a person living → choose a scarce commitment → see immediate action and a delayed consequence → encounter a contextual interruption → adapt → keep a memory → compare another path. Preserve pause, save and a clear stopping point at reviews. No streak punishment, pressure to return, paid rescue or deliberately obscured odds.

## Human attributes: what research permits

- **Disposition can persist and change.** [Roberts, Walton and Viechtbauer's longitudinal meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16435954/) finds different age trends across traits, including adult changes. More directly, [Stieger and colleagues' 2021 randomized trial](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2017548118), involving 1,523 participants and a three-month digital intervention, reports intended personality change. Neither supplies five-year game coefficients or a universal trajectory. Keep starting disposition, current traits and temporary emotional state separate; initial traits are not immutable DNA readings.
- **Heritability is variation in a population.** [Polderman and colleagues' 2015 twin-study synthesis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25985137/) concerns sources of differences between people. It does not provide a genetic percentage for an individual, a birth-to-death forecast, or justification for a fixed brain-versus-body budget. The ten-point constraint is a game rule; never present it as biology.
- **Big Five, reasoning and emotional skills are different constructs.** [Mayer, Salovey and Caruso](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18793038/) distinguish emotion-related abilities from broad bundles of desirable personality traits. [Davies, Stankov and Roberts' three studies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9825531/) expose measurement and discriminant-validity problems. Do not calculate IQ/EQ directly from Big Five sliders. Name game gauges reasoning and emotional skills, explain their authored effects, and distinguish them from standardized assessments.
- **Empathy is not a morality meter.** [Decety and Cowell](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24972506/) distinguish emotional sharing, concern and perspective-taking and describe ways empathy can guide or interfere with judgment. Model a caring response and an ethical decision separately. Do not label low empathy evil or assign moral worth to religion, sex or class.
- **Traits can suggest a challenge, not prohibit a career.** [Zell and Lesick's synthesis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34687041/) finds modest associations that vary by performance category. That cannot justify “high agreeableness means you should not be a lawyer.” Offer several routes through a major or occupation, with training, access, interests and context mattering; no trait-based career bans.
- **A wellbeing score is not dopamine.** [Schultz, Dayan and Montague](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9054347/) describe reward-prediction signals in dopaminergic neurons. That is not a method for deriving a human neurotransmitter level from wealth, choices or a simulation. Label the gauge authored wellbeing. A reference average must identify its dataset/cohort/year, or explicitly say simulated cohort; do not fabricate a population mean.

These boundaries do not make Life clinically validated. Any disease, substance-use, violence or mortality model needs separate domain evidence and calibration. Do not encode assault as a deserved consequence, psychosis as suppression of instinct, or drugs as a creativity shortcut. Hard events should have context, aftermath, care options and content controls; prevalence and effects cannot be invented to make a graph dramatic.

## Research limits and next validation

This pass found usable creator sources for The Sims, Reigns, Citizen Sleeper and Disco Elysium. Crusader Kings III's official stress announcement was discovered, but its full diary was not readable; no detailed algorithm is inferred from it. No sufficiently documented BitLife developer source was verified in this pass. 1in8b gameplay/source observations belong in the separate original-game study, with tested branches and untested branches explicitly listed.

First instrument a complete run for event eligibility, option effects, repeated events, unresolved relationships and memory provenance. Then have players compare two branches and explain consequences in their own words. Use that evidence to tune pacing and comprehension; do not claim the source studies validate Life's learning benefits or forecast the player's real future.
