## Finance methodology

The reference basket starts half in each scenario fund and is held without fees or further contributions. It describes fund prices, not the player's personal return. The personal ledger keeps actual trades and fees. Historical choices remain intact; an obsolete unresolved invitation can clear without changing balances.

There is no guaranteed recovery, fixed investing-win percentage, arbitrary retirement fine for choosing spending, or invented inflation withdrawal from a nominal cash balance.

Sources for these qualifications: [Investor.gov diversification](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/save-and-invest/diversify-your-investments) and [asset allocation](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/asset-allocation).
