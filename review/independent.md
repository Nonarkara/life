# Independent prototype review

Verdict: approved as a bounded first playable prototype.

Read-only source review found a player marker detached from its character, room occupants anchored below the floor, a rain narrative inconsistent with earnings, unexplained subtraction redistribution, and inaccurate methodology wording. Re-inspection confirms these five issues are corrected in current source. The rear tree now renders before the house.

Visually inspected updated `390.png` (390 × 892), `1024.png` (1024 × 900), and `1440.png` (1440 × 900): no visible clipping, overlapping controls, disconnected architecture, or obstructed primary action. The scene reads coherently as a small isometric diorama. Character and furniture details are sparse at this size; this supports a prototype, not a claim of upgraded realistic graphics. The allocation hint is corrected in all three captures. Desktop layouts leave considerable space below the diorama, especially at 1024 pixels; this is a minor composition issue, not an obstruction. No spatial overlap or floating-character defect is apparent in these captures.

The model's four-week arithmetic and fixed-event replay are internally consistent. Renderer depth ordering remains authored rather than general-purpose; moving characters or adding buildings will require further occlusion checks. No claim is made that a full life simulation, collision engine, or realistic world has been validated.
