## Demonstrated source defects/gaps

1. **Vocation contradicts completed choices.** routine() accepts only goal, ignoring actual pathway. A15-year apprentice is rendered going to school;20-year college student with balance goal goes to work; athlete/art goal overrides salaried/college route regardless of recorded period. Separate education/work obligation from aspiration and select activities from actual recorded/current stage context.
2. **Player overlaps static workplace NPC.** Adult generic work endpoint11.3,5.8 versus static NPC11.4,5.7: distance≈.14 world units, projecting nearly on top of each other. Existing test checks only player versus father12.4,5.8. Test every visible actor pair/work slot.
3. **Activities mostly share one gesture.** care/work/garden use the same sinusoidal reaching; play has no specific object, target or action. Drawing at6.8,5.5 is distant from desk footprint6..6.7,3.6..4.5; pencil gestures into free space. School study holds a book outdoors with no supported sitting/desk transition. Name changes alone do not make believable activities.
4. **Facing direction unused.** Route dx/dy are calculated but person() ignores them. The character keeps one orientation for every direction and return trip, producing sliding/backward walking. Locomotion leg cycles do not reflect actor speed/distance; all actors share the same phase.
5. **Ageless extras.** Two static child NPCs remain child-sized from6 through80. Mother/father have fixed adult proportions until disappearance; only player shirt==amber gets grey hair, and size jumps abruptly at10. A cohort/role policy should distinguish peers aging with the protagonist from intentionally changing neighborhood children.
6. **Repetition is structural.** Every day shares exact8/20/36/48 phase boundaries; everyone eats/rests together, whole mornings seated on bare floor, no transitions into support surfaces. Identical busy-wave/blink clocks strengthen robotic synchronization. Provide deterministic role/age schedule offsets and meaningful dwell sequences; avoid random jitter.
7. **Renderer limits graphics.** World normally draws560px-wide buffer then enlarges; surfaces are solid polygons, trees stacked boxes, shadow/contact/lighting mostly absent. Higher-resolution output alone will not resolve material or activity ambiguity. Keep palette but introduce consistent light, contact shadows, restrained surface texture and explicit prop anchors.
8. **Occlusion is approximate.** Dynamic objects sort by x+y but buildings use arbitrary hardcoded depths; traffic renders last above every object. Screen-place labels clamp to edges instead of resolving collisions, so offscreen positions can imply wrong places. New detailed geometry needs a shared ordering/visibility contract, not more unrelated hardcoded depth constants.
9. **Some tests prove obsolete paths.** layout.test.mjs rectangular parent walk is no longer renderer's daily route. Current routine furniture tests are useful but omit NPC-NPC overlap, parent/player collider separation, prop reach/facing, primary building/wall clearance and detailed rig ground contacts. Passing71 tests is not proof of believable motion.

- Keep opening womb/infant/family, current palette/amber agency, every earned world feature, console tabs, language/text sizes and approved pacing. No file collapse >30% without owner approval.
- Pure activity selector consumes age plus actual pathway, completed aim and role; test school/apprentice/college/work/independent/retired cases. Aspirations may choose discretionary activity, not erase obligations.
- Author visible actors/anchors/colliders in one layout contract. Sample entire days across ages1,3,6,10,15,20,25,45,55,60,70,80 and all pathways. Actor radius clears walls/solid furniture/new buildings; work/activity slots do not overlap. Explicit temporary crossing must be handled, not certified away by endpoint-only tests.
- Foot-contact rig invariant: planted foot stays on ground/support plane during stance; gait displacement follows traveled distance; facing follows velocity with bounded transition. Seated hips attach to intended surface, hands reach props/targets, no free-floating cups/books/tools.
- Validate distinct pose trajectories for reading, writing/art, stall preparation/service, vocational/manual work, sport, gardening, care, eating and rest. Pause yields stable frame; reduced motion shows a meaningful static pose, not missing actor/action.
- Stable age proportions, clothes/hair cues and cohort appearance policy; deceased parents absent only when recorded event/age contract says so. Labels/captions reflect same actor, viewed age and activity as render.
- Camera respects safe rectangles for dock, phone world region and portrait tablet; infant/player/activity target visible. No geometry teleport/jump during routine phase transitions; chapter changes use a deliberate transition.

- Test actual renderer ordering at known crossings: person behind/in front of wall/tree/stall, traffic passing footpaths, indoors versus outdoors, roof cutaway and growth buildings. Inspect screenshots AND short uninterrupted clips; automated coordinate checks alone cannot assess weight, mood or taste.
