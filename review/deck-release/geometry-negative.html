Rules, sources & what this prototype leaves out

This prototype uses authored game rules. Its money, health changes and event odds are not measured population statistics.

The graphs join five-year snapshots. Markers sit at the recorded review age, not an exact event date.

Ten points are a game constraint. Reasoning, empathy and strength are not biological opposites.

Smoking has costs in this model, without guaranteed happiness. Beliefs have no universal morality score.

The neighbourhood is metaphorical. Nearby buildings represent parts of a life, not a real map.

Marriage and family milestones follow your choices. Dengue, job loss and pregnancy are not yet generated by this model.
