About this economy

Source: 65 annual observations; 3 contraction spells. This does not establish a cycle.

This saved life joins the economy at age 35. Earlier records stay unchanged.

A GDP contraction is not the same as a banking crisis. This scenario samples recorded growth and keeps contraction spells together; it does not schedule a crisis every few years.

Growth observations: Thailand, World Bank, 1961–2025. The 1997–98 contraction is one spell. Fund returns are separately authored; GDP is not a stock-market return.

Your investments are valued only after you own them. Cash can pay an unexpected bill without selling a fund. Losses, fees, missed income and starting conditions still matter.

The sampler favors some years and crisis starts. Its crisis frequency is not the frequency measured in Thailand.

Historical GDP samples; fictional calendar. Wage effects and fund returns are game rules, not predictions.

Lesson reference funds start half in each scenario fund and are held without fees or new contributions. They describe a price path, not your personal return.

Rules, sources & what this prototype leaves out

This prototype uses authored game rules. Its money, health changes and event odds are not measured population statistics.

The graphs join five-year snapshots. Markers sit at the recorded review age, not an exact event date.

Ten points are a game constraint. Reasoning, empathy and strength are not biological opposites.

Smoking has costs in this model, without guaranteed happiness. Beliefs have no universal morality score.

The neighbourhood is metaphorical. Nearby buildings represent parts of a life, not a real map.

Marriage and family milestones follow your choices. Dengue, job loss and pregnancy are not yet generated by this model.
