# Original-system study — 23 October 2026

Source: https://1in8b.life/en/ v0.8.22 beta. Official UI and instructions, not its claims independently validated. The supplied ThePeople article and morluto/rea repository were also read; REA is reverse-engineering tooling, not the original game's source.

Browser evidence: resumed Deepak Joshi, India2026, age0; personally selected the age2 caring-for-another-child option and saw the consequent record. Exercised16×, next-decision, automatic decisions, pause, spending sheet, More(20), Ways of living, and half-within-means wedding/funeral gifts. Followed automated decisions through education, engineering work, job loss/reemployment, marriage, children, mortgage/debt, retirement/reemployment and migration to a child's home at87. No real payment, posting or sharing was performed. Auto choices are explicitly distinguished from personal choices.

Keep the good wheel:

| Observed pattern | Value to Life | Adaptation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Birth context remains in identity record | Circumstance stays attached to outcomes | No fabricated172-country data |
| Choice cards state expected gains/costs | Understand a choice before committing | Avoid guaranteed health/happiness claims |
| Record pairs decision and subsequent outcome | Causality can be inspected | Distinguish correlation and outside events |
| Cash ledger explains annual change | A falling line has a readable cause | Life keeps five-year snapshots for now |

| Event-marked yearly grid + survival comparison | Compact reflection with context | No invented life tables or universal mortality countdown |
| Spending by purpose, with price and credit state | Decisions connect resources to relationships | Do not copy arbitrary returns or medical effect sizes |

Scope: a substantial single-life run is not an exhaustive test of all eras, countries, decisions, diseases, investment outcomes or endings. No claim of exhausting all good features.
