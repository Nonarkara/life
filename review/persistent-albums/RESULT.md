# Persistent albums — evidence and limits

Completed life summaries now retain a bounded album of completed CV periods, milestones, activities, journey stops and encountered authored field notes. The existing browser-only lives archive remains capped at40 records. Intake exposes all retained records, including those older than the previous six-row display limit. Opening albums does not change the active save or award resources.

Identity now uses a deterministic, object-key-order-independent, versioned fingerprint of the completed record, including major choices, periods, financial records, journeys and completed activities. The original fingerprint is retained solely for migration. Reopening the one old ending still available can attach its actual album and upgrade its archive/counter keys without changing its note/date or adding taste weights/life counts again. Older summaries whose source records are gone remain readable but explicitly lack albums. An old fingerprint collision cannot be reconstructed: the source still on the device is the only album available for migration; distinct old colliding runs cannot be recovered.

The new identity fixes future collisions caused by omitted input fields; it is not a cryptographic identifier. Old born-again links continue to reproduce starting conditions, not an entire past run. Future/pending activities do not become completed receipts. Malformed optional albums are ignored without deleting valid summaries; storage write failure returns the persisted archive rather than a phantom newly saved life.

New six tests cover persisted receipts/notes, active-save replacement, record differences at equal final metrics, stable JSON round-trips, one-time legacy migration across archive/taste/Almanac, invalid optional albums, quota failures, unfinished lives, and road-stop registry totals.
