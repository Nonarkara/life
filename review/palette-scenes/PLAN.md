# Event-driven colour direction

The life, rather than a user setting, chooses its colours. Assign documented
Palette combinations to every current scenario and event type; pressure uses
cool/dark combinations, relief/connection uses brighter chords, ordinary periods
use quieter ones. Colour is atmosphere, not a medical diagnosis or moral score.

Preserve saved lives, accepted artwork, layouts and all supported languages.
Remove the manual picker, ignore legacy pins, keep language/text/motion/world
settings. The scene and UI read the same current chord. Historical viewing must
not reveal future incidents or lose a choice when it moves into the life record.
No timer-driven carousel; frequency follows actual decisions and incidents.

Ground/ink/structure/action roles remain named and contrast-tested on their actual
surfaces, including the distinct stage bar and transport keys. Darkest chord
member is reading ink against the lightest; dark instruments invert this pair.
Record source membership and licence, full scenario table, real seeded life trace,
30-chord contrast checks, actual desktop/tablet/phone scenes and native settings.
Independent source and pixel review closes findings before main publication.
