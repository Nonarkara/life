# Contextual turns in a life

Authorized scope: the owner's request for a comprehensive, replayable life game,
continued under the active release goal. Existing research/mechanics-evidence.md
and hands-on original-game study supply prior art: context-filtered choices,
recurring people, delayed outcomes and a readable life record.

Invariant: a pending decision resolves once, changes only the current recorded
snapshot, and cannot rewrite childhood or spend the same cost twice.

Preserve the world, childhood, five-year allocation, settings, charts, replay,
local saves and Palette. No source collapse or new control dashboard.

Changes:
- New encounters.js: authored eligibility, once-per-life/cooldown rules, named
  recurring people, option effects and follow-up flags. Not real-world odds.
- sim.js: offer one eligible encounter after a period; require resolution before
  the next period; keep optional older-save story records compatible.
- game.js/index/settings: current decision occupies the existing reading panel,
  compact choices beside known costs; then return to the five-year review.
- events/personality: preserve earlier markers and cohort progression; comparison
  paths resolve their own encounters and capacity histories include learned skills.
- translations: all new player-facing phrases in five languages.
- tests: context rejection, no repetition, delayed follow-ups, exact cost once,
  immutable starting state, complete runs, bad save rejection and replay.

Verification: real browser play through friendship, project and adult choices;
phone/tablet/desktop plus enlarged text and translated UI; independent source
and rendered review; build, commit, push and updated private review deployment.
The public custom-domain release remains the full objective, not this subtask.
