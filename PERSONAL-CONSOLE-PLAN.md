# Personal console, places, and sound

25 October 2026. Owner requests three clear dashboards, denser use of laptop
space, royalty-free music/mute, residence-aware expanding world, and better art.
Standing session authorization covers implementation and release. No new approval
flow is introduced. The latest screenshot rejects the previous dock composition.

## Preserve
Keep all live canvases, charts, existing controls, earned CV/history, five languages,
local saves and deterministic life rules. No file collapse over30%. Existing
rendering remains the fallback; current Three.js renderer is upgraded in place.

## Composition
Laptop: persistent world beside a compact current action; three aligned consoles
across the lower band: OCEAN personality, current condition, and five-year intent.
Each number has one visible home. The former duplicated footer HUD is moved into
condition, not deleted. Point controls become a tight five-row editor with meaningful
practice previews; large narrative/focus prose becomes a compact chapter summary.
No document scroll at common laptop sizes. Long records/settings use contained,
explicit scrolling or a detail view. Phone retains a meaningful world and one
selected section; a compact console selector minimizes scrolling.

## Proposed changes
- New dashboard module: reuse existing personality/capacities/history. Ikigai is
  explicitly an authored purpose/alignment indicator, not a clinical score or
  measured Japanese cultural construct. Research receipt defines its bounds.
- console.js/console.css: three readable panels, clear current vs planned labels,
  compact attached controls, retained advanced profile/money/history access.
- music.js: original locally synthesized CC0 instrumental score, explicit gesture
  to start, compact Music/Mute control, page-hidden suspension, no game-clock effects.
- locations.js/routines.js/finance.js: authoritative housing and work district;
  rented housing changes the actual home origin. A city work district extends the
  world, with explicit pay/travel assumptions and no instant money on selection.
  Legacy saves default to their recorded family home/local work. Age gates remain.
- scene3d.js/character3d.js/world.js: connected districts, furnished independent
  homes, workplace-specific activities, better silhouettes/material/lighting detail,
  camera clarity and preserved architectural cutaways. Parents stay in their home.
- game.js/i18n/translations: integrate sound, location captions and all new phrases.
- Focused tests: actual housing origin/return, city workplace and money conservation,
  legacy saves, bounded/reproducible metrics and pure intentions, mute/preferences.

## Validation and limits
Exercise real UI at1440x900,1366x768,1024x768,390x844 and portrait iPad; enlarged
Thai and all language mappings. Inspect sparse and adult/dense states, individual
panel overflow, body boundaries, audio gesture/mute, independent home→city work→
independent home, pause/reduced motion and existing ending graphs. Independent
source/rendered review, then commit/push/Cloudflare deploy and verify the complete
versioned public graph plus actual public UI. No claim of universal zero scrolling
for unlimited text magnification; readable contained details take precedence.
No claim that a life game is objectively superior in every perspective without
repeated human playtests. This is a substantive release toward that objective.
