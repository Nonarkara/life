## Decisions and sequence
1. Preserve liked palette, birth/family animation and current pace. Make the
   distinction between abilities, circumstances and chosen goals explicit.
2. Add a compact optional six-needs focus to the existing Profile screen. Keep
   vocation/aim separate: an athlete may seek recognition, connection or growth.
   Record focus with each five-year decision and show relevant actual evidence
   at the next review. No automatic IQ, dopamine, wellbeing or genetic changes
   from choosing a label. Old saves/aims remain valid. No second dashboard.
3. Further work: auditable shared economic-world context before investment offers;
   real holdings/exposures before gains/losses; relocation with actual costs and
   records; relationships with reciprocal support and uncertainty; reflection that
   compares actual alternatives rather than claiming what would have saved a life.
4. Inaction: explicit defer/default outcomes must be visible and recorded. An
   opt-in timed mode can come later; no silent expiry while reading or off-tab.
   Player feedback says comprehension matters and existing pace is acceptable.
