# Bedroom plate replacements, 29 October 2026

Three of Codex's 14 bedroom plates were rejected on review, and their replacements were
generated locally with FLUX.1-schnell (mflux 0.24, 4 steps, 2016×864, three seeds
each: 11, 23, 37). The prompts are in art/missing-art-requests.json, with "no watermark, no
signature, no copyright mark, no logo, no text" and "no figures, no silhouettes" added.
Candidates are in scratch/anime/candidates/flux.

| Plate | Why the original was rejected | Pick | Why the others were rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| bedroom-night@tower | a "© 2018" band baked into the bottom-left corner | seed 23 | seed 11 was usable; 23 has the clearer view of the housing block |
| bedroom-day@newengland | a person standing at the window | seed 23 | 11 and 37 have snow piled inside the room |
| bedroom-night@slum | a resort room with a sea view | seed 23 | 11 is a resort pool deck; 37 has a lettered poster |

All three picks were checked edge to edge for marks or lettering, and none were found.
Converted as prepare_plate does: 1760 px wide, WebP quality 68.

The grandmother sheet was regenerated three times, and all three were rejected: missing
panels, loose fragments (a skirt alone, a leg alone), children and pots in the frame, and
a different woman in each pose. Her crouching pose stays withheld, and a life that needs
it keeps the 3D world.
